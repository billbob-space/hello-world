## Priority Issues

### [P0] On narrow screens with the fiche open, five nodes fire a different command
`cadre()` subtracts the fiche's height at the bottom but never the toolbar's at the top, so the ring rides up under `#barre`. Verified by pointer test at each pastille centre: `Air→#ouvrir-palmares`, `Björk→#ouvrir-collection`, `Moby→#ouvrir-collection`, `PJ Harvey→#graine`, `Death in Vegas→.barre`. Björk is a *branch* — a primary target. This is not a missed tap; it is a wrong action executed silently on the product's founding gesture, reached by the ordinary sequence of opening the fiche to read and then choosing where to go next.
**Fix.** In `cadre()`, subtract the toolbar's height on narrow and offset `cy` into the genuinely free space rather than centring in a rectangle with an occupied edge.

### [P0] Canvas nodes have no illustration fallback — all 28 break together
`fond()` probes real load failure for HTML tiles; `dessineNoeud()` only checks that the URL parses, then points `fill` at a `<pattern>`. With images blocked: **28 pastilles, 28 using `url(#motif)`, 0 falling back, 0 initials** — every node renders as a browser broken-image glyph, centre included, with parent links visibly crossing empty circles. §11 requires that no missing illustration leaves a void; this is the one screen the PRD calls "the product", and it is the only place the fallback does not exist. `cdn-images.dzcdn.net` appears on common blocklists, so this is an ordinary production condition.
**Fix.** Probe with `new Image()` in `dessineNoeud()` exactly as `fond()` does, and on error swap the fill to `teinteRepli()` and append the initials text that already exists directly below.

### [P1] The failed-plant screen is a cul-de-sac with four compounding faults
(a) Both failure kinds share the title "L'arbre s'arrête ici", though F-36 — marked **Critique** — demands two different messages. (b) The body advises "choisis une suggestion" while the overlay is `position:fixed; inset:0; z-index:40`, so `#graine` is unreachable. (c) The overlay has **no `role`, no `aria-modal`, no `tabindex`, and sets nothing `[inert]`** — all 28 tiles beneath are pointer-blocked but **still tabbable**, and the single exit button is reached only after 29 stops. (d) Returning home does not clear `#graine`, so the next attempt concatenates: verified `"PortishedKzzzqqwwxx"`.
**Fix.** Two titles. Put a live search field inside the box for the not-found case, pre-filled and selected, with clickable suggestions from `api/suggestions` (which already returns Portishead first for "portis"). Give the overlay `role="alertdialog"` + `aria-modal`, move focus into it, and mark the rest inert. Clear `#graine` on return home.

### [P1] WCAG 2.2 AA still fails on the canvas, on two measurable criteria
**Targets:** héritier pastilles measure **17px** diameter with the fiche open (21px collapsed) against SC 2.5.8's 24px floor — 12 of 18 fail. The label does not help: `.noeud__nom` carries `pointer-events:none`. B's 32×45 figure measured the `<g>` bounding box, which that non-interactive label inflates; the real tappable region is the circle.
**Non-text contrast:** `--vitre #2f6577` is the sole visual boundary of every transparent-background control — **2.75:1 / 2.40:1 / 2.15:1**, under SC 1.4.11's 3:1. `.noeud__pastille`'s stroke measures 2.75:1. I raised the text tokens and `--trait` and stopped before the controls.
**Fix.** Raise the héritier radius floor in `rayons()` from 8 to 13, or add an invisible `r=13` hit circle. Introduce a `--bord-commande` at ≥3:1 for `.bouton`, `.filtre` and the selects; leave `--vitre` to panel edges.

### [P2] Two orientation debts carried over untouched
Tab order still crosses 18 héritiers before 9 branches — 19 stops to the first primary target, 28 to leave the canvas for "Garder". And `svg#scene` remains the only focus stop with **no visible indicator at all**: its `outline` is computed but the element is exactly 1440×900, so the ring draws entirely off-viewport — 0 changed pixels of 1,296,000. Also unfixed: hot resize 390→1440 still overflows by 368px, and the wall is still anonymous on desktop until hover.

## Questions to Consider

1. **The PRD bounds branches at 8–10 "beyond which the canvas becomes illegible" — you render 9 plus 18 héritiers, so 27 targets. Did the bound mean branches or nodes?** The héritiers are half the call budget, most of the label collisions, and all twelve sub-24px targets.
2. **How many first sessions end on "L'arbre s'arrête ici"?** You instrument M-01 to M-05 and measure nothing about failed plants or exits from `.etat-ecran` — the one screen everyone eventually meets.
3. **Has anyone opened the app with `cdn-images.dzcdn.net` blocked?** It is the default in several content blockers, and it is the condition under which all 28 nodes break at once.
4. **"Both layouts equally" — the tree is 80% of a desktop viewport and 35% of a phone's.** Is a full ring the right shape inside 9:19.5, or would a half-circle fan carry the same information in the space that exists?
5. **The product knows at startup what it cannot do** (`/api/diagnostic` reports appreciation absent) **and still leaves Palmarès enabled to fail after 20 seconds.** Why don't real deployment capabilities drive the interface, the way filters already hide themselves when there is nothing to filter?
