## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment.** The canvas is genuinely authored for this product; the chrome is not. État B is real information design — concentric growth rings as a cut trunk, affinity encoded simultaneously in distance *and* diameter, heir clusters fanned outward from their branch, lineage as a grafted stem, didone reserved for the centre alone. No unrelated product reuses that composition unchanged. Two reservations. The home wall — full-bleed cover grid with an overlaid title — is a pattern shared with Letterboxd, Criterion, any media catalogue; what rescues it is the committed cyanotype transfer, but a film service would adopt it wholesale by swapping posters for covers. More seriously, the declared signature element barely reaches the pixel: the rings are stroked in `--vitre` at **1.62:1** then multiplied by `opacity:.5`, and the lineage's graft scars are 1px in the same token at **1.42:1**. The thesis is stated with unusual conviction in the CSS comments and only half-delivered on screen.

**Deterministic scan.** Static `detect.mjs` returned **0 findings** across `index.html`, the `web/` directory, the CSS and the JS. B validated this rather than trusting it: a canary file with `font-family:Inter` and a bounce easing correctly returned 3 findings, so the detector works and the corpus is genuinely clean. The stale `hook.cache.json` entry for `overused-font:fraunces` is resolved — the face is now Bodoni Moda.

The injected **browser** detector found 33 (home) and 46 (exploration). B triaged these rigorously, and most are false positives: `marquee` on `body` fires on the French word *"marquee"* in a code comment (`ramure.js:20`) — there is no `<marquee>` element in the DOM; `gradient-text` fires with no `background-clip:text` anywhere in the document; `clipped-overflow-container` ×29 is legitimate thumbnail cropping; `text-occlusion` on the rings legend names `svg.scene` as the occluder, which has `pointer-events:none` and sits *below* the legend. The survivors are real and corroborate A: **`low-contrast` ×2** and **`undersized-ui-text` ×13** (10px `.album__type`).

**Visual overlays.** Injection succeeded (`addScriptTag` → `impeccableDetectAsync` present, mutation probe passed), but the browser ran headless with no human tab, so there is no user-visible overlay to look at — the console findings above are the deliverable. B correctly re-ran with `autoScan:false` after discovering the detector was detecting its own 63 injected badges.

## Overall Impression

The engine underneath this interface is better than the interface. The three-state model (ok / empty / failure) is carried structurally from Go through to two distinct JS error classes and separate buttons; reduced motion is genuinely neutralised (a promotion takes **169ms** vs 480ms, because the script short-circuits the wait rather than letting CSS shorten one animation); there are zero duplicate accessible names across eight measured surface states. That is careful work.

And two of the three entry paths are broken. Plant a misspelled name from the home screen and **nothing happens** — no message, no spinner, no announcement, and the field empties so there is nothing left to correct. On touch, the wall that is supposed to "donner envie" shows anonymous photographs with no names, and 4 of 10 tiles cannot be clicked at all because the banner intercepts the pointer. The single biggest opportunity is not a new feature: it is making the excellent state machine that already exists actually reach the screen.

## What's Working

**The three-state model refuses to collapse into a boolean.** `Vide` and `Panne` are distinct JS classes carried from the server and treated separately all the way to the button. Verified live: from the canvas, an unknown name gives "L'arbre s'arrête ici" *without* retry; a network cut gives "Chargement impossible" *with* retry *and* an exit, with the previous 28 nodes still behind the overlay. It works because the distinction is structural, not parametric.

**The single search field is moved, not duplicated.** `hote.appendChild($("recherche"))` relocates one node between the home banner and the toolbar. B measured 0 duplicate accessible names across 8 state/viewport combinations, and found exactly one `input#graine` at both 390 and 1440. This is a §12 constraint solved by architecture instead of discipline — a media-query pair would leave both in the accessibility tree.

**Reduced motion is neutralised, not shortened.** Every measured transition drops to 1e-05s, `getAnimations()` is empty in both modes, and the script reads the preference at runtime to skip the promotion wait entirely. §11's actual sentence — "un utilisateur sensible au mouvement ne doit jamais attendre plus longtemps qu'un autre" — is honoured, which most implementations miss.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Empty and error states never render on the entry path
**What.** `#etat-ecran` is a child of `<section class="exploration" hidden>`. Until `montreArbre()` calls `basculeVers("exploration")`, that section stays hidden, and the `[hidden]{display:none!important}` rule — added deliberately so `hidden` always wins — annihilates the state box. Measured on `?graine=Zqxwvbnmqq`: `#etat-titre` contains "L'arbre s'arrête ici", `etat-ecran.hidden === false`, and `getBoundingClientRect().height === 0`. Same for the waiting spinner and for `panne`. Independently verified: `montreEtat()` contains no `basculeVers`, and `alerte(` has exactly one occurrence in the file — its own definition.
**Why it matters.** This is 100% of first sessions and 100% of shared links, which §03 calls the product's only acquisition channel. The PRD marks F-36 and F-38 **Critique**; both fail here, and F-37 is moot because you cannot retry what you cannot see. Nothing is announced either, so screen-reader users cannot detect it at all.
**Fix.** Move `#etat-ecran` out of `section.exploration` to `<main>` level, or call `basculeVers("exploration")` at the top of `montreEtat()`. Wire `alerte()` to fire on every state so the dead `role="alert"` region works. Stop clearing the search field before a tree has actually been obtained.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] The entry wall is anonymous on touch, and partly unclickable everywhere
**What.** Three compounding defects. (a) `.tuile__nom{opacity:0}` is revealed only by `:hover`/`:focus-visible`; no `@media (hover:none)` restores it — measured opacity **0** at 390×844 where `hover:none` and `pointer:coarse` are both true. (b) The banner intercepts pointer events: `elementFromPoint` shows **5 of 28** tiles at 1440 and **4 of 10** at 390 are captured by `p.marque__cri`, `div.bandeau__identite` or `header.bandeau`. Independent proof — `page.click('#mur > button:nth-child(1)')` times out after 30s with Playwright logging `<p class="marque__cri"> … intercepts pointer events`. `.mur__voile` correctly carries `pointer-events:none`; `.bandeau` does not. (c) `role="listitem"` on `<button>` overrides the native role on all 28 tiles.
**Why it matters.** §07 states "chaque tuile est une action explicite : planter cet artiste". On touch it is neither explicit nor identified; under the banner it is unclickable; to a screen reader it is not announced as a button at all (WCAG 4.1.2). The wall is the entry screen's whole bet.
**Fix.** Add `pointer-events:none` to `.bandeau` and restore it on its own interactive children, or start the grid below the banner. Show `.tuile__nom` persistently at reduced opacity under `(hover:none)`. Drop `role="listitem"` (and the paired `role="list"`) and let 28 buttons be buttons.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`

### [P1] `--brume` fails AA on every surface it touches
**What.** One token, eight measured failures. `#56736f` against `--nuit` = **3.43:1**, against `--sous-bois` = **3.04:1**, against `--taillis` = **3.05:1**; AA small text needs 4.5:1. Affected: the search placeholder, `.tri__etiquette`, `.fiche__audience`, `.disco__titre`, `.album__meta`, `.album__type` (also only **10px**), and the legend's "Lointain". The browser detector independently flagged two of these as `low-contrast`. Separately, `--vitre` at 1.42–1.62:1 carries the rings and the parent-links — the objects that *are* the affinity encoding — well under the 3:1 that SC 1.4.11 requires of meaningful graphics.
**Why it matters.** §12 targets WCAG 2.2 AA "sans exception sur l'écran principal". The rings being near-invisible is also why a legend is needed to explain an encoding the form was supposed to convey alone.
**Fix.** The correct values already exist in the file: `@media (prefers-contrast: more)` raises `--vitre` to `#6d8f9c` and `--brume` to `#9fbdb2`. Promote those to defaults. A system preference almost nobody enables cannot carry conformance, and both stay inside the cyanotype family.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable colorize`

### [P1] On wide screens the fiche cannot be closed and buries the camera controls
**What.** `.fiche__poignee{display:none}` above 900px, and the `.fiche__replier` button the CSS positions at `ramure.css:1246` **exists in no file**. `.camera-outils` (z-index 15) sits entirely beneath `#fiche` (z-index 25); `elementFromPoint` over the zoom buttons returns `album__titre`. On narrow, `basculeFiche()` measures occupied height with `setTimeout(rend, 40)` while the transition runs 480ms — measured `--place-fiche: 142px` while the fiche actually occupies 523px, so the centre node ends up behind its own fiche.
**Why it matters.** F-17 requires "retour au cadrage neutre en une action" — unreachable by mouse. §07 promises the canvas re-frames when a panel opens or collapses; it never does.
**Fix.** Add the `.fiche__replier` button the CSS already expects; shift `.camera-outils` to `right: calc(23rem + 2rem)` while the fiche is open on wide; replace the 40ms timeout with a `transitionend` listener plus a 520ms fallback.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

### [P2] Canvas label geometry — real, but a third of what I claimed
**What.** Measured at rest (B invalidated its own first pass, which had been taken after 40 tab presses that panned the camera): **1 colliding label pair at 1440×900** (26×0.5px, 1% of the smaller box) and **2 pairs at 390×844** (6.5×5.5px = 10%, and 0.5×9px = 1%). Labels drawn over *another node's pastille*: 4 occurrences at 1440, **24 at 390**. Critically, every `.noeud__nom` carries `stroke:#071a24; stroke-width:3.5px; paint-order:stroke`, so a glyph's immediate background is always the paper — the halo works, and no name is actually rendered unreadable. Alongside this, **10 interactive targets fall under 44×44** at 390 (seven héritiers at ~32×45, three filter buttons at 32px high) with 7 centre-pairs closer than 44px.
**Why it matters.** §11's exact wording is that a name is never *masked by a neighbouring pastille* — the halo satisfies that. What remains is visual noise at 390px and, more seriously, **targets below WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8**, which is a conformance failure rather than an aesthetic one.
**Fix.** Reduce narrow-screen density further (4 branches, 1 héritier) so both the label crowding and the target sizing resolve together; raise `.filtre` to 44px.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`

## Minor Observations

- **The deterministic image fallback never renders.** `querySelectorAll('.tuile__repli').length === 0` even with every cover request failing — the class is chosen from URL validity, not load success, so a network failure leaves an empty tinted box. §11 requires that no missing illustration leaves a void.
- **Hot resize 390 → 1440 in exploration overflows by 368px** (`scrollWidth 1808` vs `clientWidth 1440`): the fiche keeps its bottom-sheet positioning after widening. Fresh loads at 1440 are clean.
- **No `h1` is exposed in état B** — the only one is sealed inside `section#accueil[hidden]`. No heading-level skips anywhere, in either state.
- **`fiche-bio` and `fiche-genres` are empty for every artist** in the default deployment; F-19 asks for presentation, genres, audience and illustration, and two of four are missing with nothing signalling it.
- **The empty-palmarès message blames genre coverage** ("la source d'appréciation, qui ne couvre pas tous les genres") when `/api/diagnostic` reports the role as entirely absent. Honest in tone, wrong in fact.
- **"Cette collection est enregistrée sur cet appareil uniquement" wears `.panneau__vide`** — dashed border, centred — a status note dressed as an empty state, sitting above the content it qualifies.
- **The graft scar** (`.lignee__greffe`, 18×1px in `--vitre` at 1.42:1) is the product's second declared botanical device and is invisible; the collection renders the same relationship as a plain `⋅`. Two grammars for one relation.
- **Suggestions show only name + 34px thumbnail** — adding genre or audience would defuse the homonym risk the PRD rates "élevée".

## Questions to Consider

1. **Is the home wall a proposition or a courtesy?** Twenty-eight faces with no names, four of them unclickable, several dimmed to near-black under the veil. If the entry screen must "donner envie", why does it hide the one thing that creates desire — the names?
2. **You arbitrated "both layouts equally" — does the screen bear that out?** §14 demanded the call be made *before* interface design. The measured consequence is 24 label-over-pastille occurrences at 390px, seven sub-44px targets, and a centre node hidden behind its own fiche. Is "equally" the elegant way of not having decided?
3. **What is a signature element you cannot see?** The rings are stroked at 1.62:1 and then halved in opacity. If affinity really reads from position on the rings, why are the rings the least visible object on screen — and why does a legend have to say what the form was supposed to say alone?
4. **Does Palmarès deserve one of four mobile toolbar slots** when, in the deployment the README calls nominal, it is structurally empty? Filters hide themselves when there is nothing to filter (F-22); why doesn't this?
5. **How many of your critical requirements are verified by a test that looks at the screen?** §13 says the costliest defects pass compilation and unit tests. The P0 here is exactly that: `arbre_test.go` proves branch angles never collide, and the empty state still never rendered.
